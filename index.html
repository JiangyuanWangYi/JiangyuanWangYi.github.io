Current Research

Rejecting Extraneous Fragments in Multi-Object 3D Reassembly

In Progress
Independent research direction

Given a mixed pool of fragments from multiple broken objects, most reassembly methods (e.g. GARF, CRAG) assume every fragment belongs to one clean object. This project introduces a fragment-level matching confidence head, trained with cross-object contrastive negatives on top of PTv3-based fracture features, to identify and reject extraneous fragments before reassembly begins.

Fractured Object Instance Segmentation in 3D Scenes

Exploratory
Bridging fragment geometry with scene-level segmentation

Investigating fractured object detection inside indoor 3D scenes — relaxing the reassembly community's assumption of clean, isolated fragments by combining Breaking Bad–style fragment geometry with ScanNet-style scene context.
